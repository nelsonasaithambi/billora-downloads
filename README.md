You need a Billora account to sign in. **Accounts are created on our website:** <https://billora.co.in/billora-pos/signup/>
(14-day free trial). You can also use Billora in a browser at <https://web.billora.co.in>.

## Installing

**Windows:** unzip the file into a folder and run `Billora.Desktop.exe`. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show
"Windows protected your PC": choose **More info → Run anyway**.

**Android:** open the `.apk` on your phone. The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android will ask you to allow installing from
this source. To print, pair your Bluetooth printer in Android settings first, then choose it in the app under *Receipt printer*.

## Check your download (optional)

Each release lists SHA-256 checksums (`SHA256SUMS.txt`). On Windows: `Get-FileHash Billora-Desktop.zip -Algorithm SHA256`.

## Renewing

If your trial or subscription has ended, the app shows a **Renew** link, or go to <https://billora.co.in/billora-pos/renew/>.
